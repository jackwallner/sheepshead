Learn Sheepshead without holding up the table. Sheepshead Trainer breaks the five-player game into short drills on trump, picking, the bury and trick play, and every answer explains the reasoning so the rule sticks.

Practice alone at your own pace. There are no opponents, no timers unless you want one, and no account to create.

FOUR FREE ROOMS

The Card Room
Get to know the 32-card deck: which cards are trump, what each card is worth, the two-card blind, picking, and how the called ace makes a partner.

The Trump Room
Look at a holding and decide what to notice first. Sort the queens, jacks and diamonds from the fail suits, and keep trick strength and point value apart.

The Bury Room
Take the blind, then choose two cards to bury from an eight-card holding. Compare your pick with the coach, who explains short suits, banked points and hold cards.

The Trick Room
Make the call on following suit, trump order, finding your partner and catching the points that decide whether the picker reaches 61.

HOW IT FEELS
Swipe through flashcards and flip them for the explanation. Choice questions grade the moment you tap. Bury holdings let you pick two cards and see the coach's plan. A daily streak keeps you coming back.
