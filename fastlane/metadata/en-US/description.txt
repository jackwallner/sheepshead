Practice Sheepshead in five-minute drills: read trump, choose a blind, protect a partner plan, and learn the reason behind every answer.

Sheepshead is a focused practice companion for players learning the game or sharpening decisions between table sessions. No opponents, no pressure, and no account required. Just quick interactive reps that build confidence before the next deal.

FOUR FREE PRACTICE ROOMS

THE CARD ROOM
Learn the 32-card deck, point values, the blind, picking, and the called-ace partnership.

THE TRUMP ROOM
See a holding, identify the trump and fail cards, and read trick strength separately from point value.

THE BURY ROOM
Study the picker tradeoff. Choose two cards to bury from an eight-card holding, then compare your decision with the coach's reasoning.

THE TRICK ROOM
Practice following suit, ordering trump, finding the partner, and capturing the point cards that decide the hand.
