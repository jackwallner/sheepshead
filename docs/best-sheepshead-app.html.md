## Sheepshead, the App

Sheepshead, the App is the most-rated Sheepshead app on the iPhone. You play against computer players drawn as friendly animals, with three-, four- or five-handed games, regular or tournament scoring, jack or ace partner, and leasters or doublers, per the listing. Reviewers like that it is quick and ad-free and that you can learn without table pressure. The trade-offs: the current version dates from early 2024, and some reviewers say the bots mishandle partner play and that deals feel lopsided.

## K9 Sheepshead: Schafkopf

K9 Sheepshead is the closest game to a teacher. The listing describes a five-player game with any mix of friends and AI bots, multiplayer, and an AI that explains each strategic decision, plus a puppy-training progression that advances as you learn. It is also updated often. The trade-offs: the puppy theme is a matter of taste, and the listing does not say which partner variants it supports.

## Sheepshead: Card Game

This newer app focuses on playing with people: private tables you share by link, quick-match into open games, and 3-, 4- and 5-player matches. The listing claims wide variant coverage (Sheepshead and Schafkopf, called ace or Jack of Diamonds, leaster, doubler or choose-on-end, cracking, Blitz and Schneider scoring), a step-by-step tutorial, four AI characters, daily challenges and ratings. The trade-off is age: it was released in 2026, so it has few ratings and a short track record.

## Sheepshead (Derek Hoffmeister)

This long-running single-player app supports three-, four- and five-handed play, called ace or Jack of Diamonds partners, optional leasters, knocking, a ten-hand tournament mode, statistics, Game Center and large-print cards. Reviewers praise rules that match home games and a developer who answers. Its rating is the lowest of the five: reviewers cite deals that feel lopsided, bots that go alone on weak hands, and freezes after updates. Check the latest reviews before relying on it.

## Sheepshead Trainer (my app)

Sheepshead Trainer has four free practice rooms: Card, Trump, Bury and Trick. You learn the 32-card deck and point values, identify trump and fail cards in a holding, choose two cards to bury from an eight-card holding and compare with the coach's reasoning, then practice following suit, ordering trump and finding the partner. There are no opponents and no account. Its limits are real. It is not a game, so you never play a hand. It focuses on the five-player called-ace game, and it is new with no ratings yet. Endless practice, Fix My Mistakes and the timed challenge need Sheepshead+.

## Questions

### What is the best Sheepshead app for iPhone?

Sheepshead, the App is the most-rated, for games against the computer. For explanations, K9 Sheepshead says it explains each strategic decision. For online tables, Sheepshead: Card Game. For practice drills, Sheepshead Trainer. The best one depends on whether you want to play or practice.

### Where can I play Sheepshead online?

In Sheepshead: Card Game or K9 Sheepshead. On iPhone and iPad, Sheepshead: Card Game lists quick-match into open online games and private tables you share by link with friends. K9 Sheepshead lists multiplayer with any mix of friends and AI bots. The other apps here are single-player against the computer, so check each listing for the current online features.

### Which Sheepshead app teaches strategy?

K9 Sheepshead says it explains each decision, Sheepshead: Card Game lists a step-by-step tutorial, and Sheepshead Trainer drills trump, picking and burying with a written reason for each answer.

### Can I choose a called ace or Jack of Diamonds partner?

Yes, in several apps. Sheepshead, the App and Sheepshead by Derek Hoffmeister both list jack or ace partner options, and Sheepshead: Card Game lists called ace and Jack of Diamonds. Sheepshead Trainer focuses on the called-ace game.

### Is Schafkopf the same as Sheepshead?

Not exactly. They are related card games. Sheepshead is the American descendant of Schafkopf, and two of these listings mention Schafkopf by name. Rules differ by region, so check that an app matches your table.
