How it's built and why it matters.

The Pitch

Most players learn sheepshead at a table, then have no way to practice until the next deal. Trump order fades, buries become guesses, and trick decisions disappear as soon as the table gets busy.

Sheepshead Trainer is practice without a table: quick, interactive reps you can run anywhere. Learn the deck, read the blind, choose two to bury, and take tricks without giving points away.

Sheepshead+

All four rooms stay free, forever. Sheepshead+ simply adds more: monthly or yearly (both with a 1-week free trial), or a one-time purchase for lifetime access. Prices are shown in the app before you buy and vary by region.

Extra practice sets. A second set of questions, hands, deals, and calls in every room, using drills you already know.

The Master Tables. Advanced bury strategy, partner defense, live-trump tracking, and expert trick play for players moving past the basics.
