Common questions

How do I restore Sheepshead+?
Open Settings in the app and choose Restore Purchases. Apple must be signed in to the account that made the purchase.

How do I cancel?
Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, then choose Sheepshead Trainer.

Is this a full sheepshead game?
No. Sheepshead is a practice companion for trump, picking, burying, and trick decisions.

Why does my score differ from another table?
House rules and counting conventions vary. Each explanation states the assumptions used by the practice item.
