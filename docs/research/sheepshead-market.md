# Sheepshead practice market notes

This is a lightweight product note for Sheepshead, not a claim of
current market size or App Store ranking. Recheck competitors and prices before
making a monetization decision.

## Position

Most sheepshead apps focus on playing complete games. Sheepshead is
deliberately narrower: short practice reps for people who already have a table
or partner but want to improve between games. The differentiator is a calm,
explanation-first loop with no opponent, timer, or account requirement in the
free rooms.

## Product implications

- Keep the Card, Trump, Bury, and Trick rooms obvious from the home
  lobby.
- Make card faces large and high contrast. Rule recognition is the product, so visual
  clarity beats decorative art.
- Use examples that expose the decision, then explain the rule and the tradeoff.
- Offer a one-time lifetime option beside subscriptions because a practice app
  has low ongoing server cost.
- Treat house-rule differences as copy and settings questions, not hidden
  assumptions.
