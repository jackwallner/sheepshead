# Practice UX notes

The swipe deck remains the signature interaction, but the lobby should read as
rooms rather than a long list of drills. A player should see what to practice,
why it matters, and how much remains in one glance.

## Rules for new rooms

- The room card names the skill in plain language.
- The first drill is usable without a purchase.
- A wrong answer stays visible with its explanation until the player advances.
- Progress counts only drills the player can currently open.
- Generated practice uses the same card and explanation components as authored
  practice.
- Every tappable card has a generous target and an accessibility label.

## Interaction choices

Flashcards are good for vocabulary and rules. Multiple choice is good when the
player needs to commit to a count or decision. Hand matching is good for the
first recognition pass. Bury scenarios deserve their own selection flow
because choosing multiple cards is different from choosing one answer.
