## Sheepshead Trainer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Sheepshead Trainer works without an account. Practice progress, streaks,
preferences, review history, and onboarding choices are stored locally
on your device and are not uploaded by the app.

## Purchases and support

Apple processes Sheepshead+ purchases. RevenueCat verifies subscription
status and may process an anonymous app user ID, transaction and
entitlement information, and limited technical context. To learn which
screens lead to a membership, the app also sends RevenueCat counts of
where and how often the Sheepshead+ offer was shown, the dates of the
first offer and of a purchase, how many times the app was opened before
the first offer, and which screen a purchase started from. It does not
receive your practice content, name, or email. See
[RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/). If you email support, we receive the information you include and use
it to answer you.

## Feedback

On iPhone and iPad, Send Feedback and the one question asked when a
membership is ending send what you write straight to the developer.
Supabase stores it. Each message also carries the app version, iOS
version, device model, language and region setting, and whether you are
a Sheepshead+ member. No name, email or account is attached. Messages are
read to fix problems and improve the app, and are kept only as long as
that takes. Because nothing ties a message to you, email us what you
sent and roughly when if you want it removed.

## Ad attribution

If you install Sheepshead Trainer from an Apple Ads search ad, Apple's
AdServices framework tells RevenueCat which campaign and keyword led to
the install. This uses no advertising identifier and does not track you
across other apps. We use it only to measure whether our ads pay for
themselves.

## What we do not collect

No account, advertising identifier, analytics SDK, tracking pixel,
location, contacts, photos, microphone, or health data. We do not sell
personal information.

## Retention and deletion

Local practice data remains until you delete it or remove the app.
Support messages are retained only as needed to answer and document the
request. Apple and RevenueCat retain transaction records under their
policies.

## Children and changes

Sheepshead Trainer is not directed to children under 13 and does not
knowingly collect personal information from children. We may update this
policy when the app or its services change.
