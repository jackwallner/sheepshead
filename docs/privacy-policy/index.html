Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Sheepshead Trainer is designed to be useful without an account and to keep your practice history on your device.

Information stored on your device

Sheepshead Trainer stores practice progress, streaks, preferences, review history, and onboarding choices locally on your device. This information is not uploaded by Sheepshead Trainer. Removing the app removes this local data, subject to the normal behavior of your device backups.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes purchases through the App Store. Sheepshead Trainer Trainer uses RevenueCat to read and manage subscription status and provide subscription reporting. RevenueCat may receive an anonymous app identifier and purchase or entitlement information. It does not receive your name or email from Sheepshead Trainer. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include in that message. We use it to answer your question and do not sell it.

What Sheepshead does not collect

  • No account or sign-up is required.
  • No advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs, or tracking pixels.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or health data.
  • No sale of personal information.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app or its services change. The date above identifies the current version. Questions can be sent to jackwallner+m@gmail.com.
