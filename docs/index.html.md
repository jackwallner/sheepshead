## Questions

## Is there an app to practice the Sheepshead card game?

Yes. Sheepshead Trainer is a free iPhone and iPad app that drills trump order, picking, burying and trick play in five-minute sessions. It is practice, not a full game, and each answer comes with the coach's reasoning.

## How do I learn Sheepshead as a beginner?

Learn the cards and their trump order first, then the roles of picker and partner, then what to bury and how to win tricks. Sheepshead Trainer follows that order across its Card, Trump, Bury and Trick rooms.

## What should I bury in Sheepshead?

Bury the points you want to keep for the picking side while keeping the trump and strength you need to play the hand. The Bury Room gives you eight-card picker holdings, then compares your bury with the coach's pick and shows the blind tradeoff.

## How do I learn the Sheepshead trump order?

Practice it until it is automatic. The Trump Room covers permanent trump, fail suits and point cards, and the Trick Room has you order the trumps and follow the led suit in real situations.

## Is Sheepshead Trainer a full game against computer opponents?

No. There are no opponents or bots. It is a practice app for decisions between games at your own table.

## Is Sheepshead Trainer free, and does it need an account?

The download is free, with four practice rooms, flashcards and streaks included, and there is no account or sign-up. An optional Sheepshead+ upgrade adds extra practice sets and the Master Tables, and prices are shown in the app before you buy.

## Does it work offline?

Yes. Every drill works with no network connection, and your progress stays on your device.
